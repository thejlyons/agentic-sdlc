# Renegotiate a costly rule with readings and one proposal

Date: 2026-11-11

Rule: Any rule set under older conditions can be changed. When a rule costs real work, keep obeying it and bring the owner the readings and one proposed replacement in the same message.

Incident: The lead obeyed a "stop at 13 GB of swap" rule through four wasted runs of one card. The rule was set when several simulators ran at once; a single cold-booted simulator parks about 11.6 GB at a load of 2.

Why: The goal is to keep improving the process, not to honour limits made for a different situation. Working around a rule silently, or obeying it forever, are both failures.

How to apply: Raise it at the first wasted run, not the fourth. Give the measured numbers and exactly one replacement rule. Apply the change the same day in the script, the skill and the briefs.
