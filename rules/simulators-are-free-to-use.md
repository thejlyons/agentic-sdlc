# Simulators and their tooling may be used freely; the memory rules stay

Date: 2026-11-07

Rule: Agents may create, boot, shut down and erase any simulator and use the device tooling without asking. The lane, the gate, the stop rule and the protected folders still apply.

Incident: Earlier rules forbade touching other apps' simulators or the device manager. They slowed work and raised permission prompts for no benefit, since the simulators exist only for this work.

Why: Restrictions that protect nothing cost time.

How to apply: Drop "do not touch other simulators" lines from briefs. Keep: one lane, the start gate, the stop rule, erase after use, and never touching a protected path. Workers still shut simulators down by UDID so they do not stop another worker's.
