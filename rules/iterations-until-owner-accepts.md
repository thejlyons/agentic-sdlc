# Taste work is an iteration until the owner accepts it

Date: 2026-11-15

Rule: For anything judged by taste, a change is one more iteration. Only the owner declares an element final.

Incident: The lead treated a shipped design as finished, copied its look to a second place and moved on, while the owner still considered the first unfinished.

Why: The owner holds the quality bar for taste work, and building on an unaccepted element multiplies the rework.

How to apply: Report "a new iteration of X is live", never done or final. Keep a status per element with two end states: iterating, or accepted by the owner on a date. Do not copy an unaccepted element to new places or stack dependent work on it. Keep proposing the next polish step. Brief workers the same way. Engineering cards are separate: there, Done still means merged with review and green CI.
