# Visual pitches need a craft brief and a render-critique-fix loop

Date: 2026-11-14

Rule: Any visual pitch is made by parallel illustrator agents, one per style family, each given the same scene, a list of banned generic tells, and a required loop of rendering, looking, critiquing and fixing.

Incident: A first round of four quick vector mockups was judged machine-made: thick uniform outlines, candy gradients, circles for everything. A second round of ten styles, made with the brief below, landed well.

Why: Generic primitives, rainbow gradients, emoji faces and uniform strokes read as machine-made, and the owner judges taste from pictures.

How to apply: Brief: the exact same scene for every option; the real frame (portrait phone for a phone product); banned tells (bare circles, rainbow or neon gradients, emoji faces, uniform strokes, drop shadows everywhere, centred-by-default); subject-correct anatomy; four to six colours and one light source per style; period-appropriate fonts, not the common defaults. Loop: headless render, open the PNG, written critique, fix; at least two rounds. Show everything on one gallery with honest pros and cons: production cost, readability at phone size, fit with the tone. Pitch more styles than the first favourites.
