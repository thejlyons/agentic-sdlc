# A question parks one card, never the queue

Date: 2026-11-11

Rule: A decision the owner must make blocks only its own card. The lead records the question, notifies once, and dispatches the next card.

Incident: Work used to halt while a question waited for the owner, who checks in every few hours.

Why: The owner's words: a decision does not need to become a blocker for everything.

How to apply: Set `needs_j` with the question and a recommendation as the first paragraph of the card's notes. Send one notification under 200 characters that leads with the decision. No second notification for the same question. Batch questions, numbered, answerable as "all yes". Visual choices are two or three real screenshots. Check for answers on every lead pass: one answer sat unhandled overnight.
