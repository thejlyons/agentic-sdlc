                How this page counts, and where it reads from
                • Needs you: cards with needs_j true that aren't built. The ones in stages 0 and 1 are shown in full with the question from the top of the card's notes; an answer you send is saved on the card as a note. Cards in the license stages and After 1.0 are folded under them.
                • The count in the top line is the stage 0 and 1 cards you haven't answered yet. It is a different number from a tile's "need you this phase", which counts needs_j cards in that app's current phase, answered or not, license stages included.
                • Since you last looked: cards whose history has an entry after the last time this device had the page open (kept in this browser only; a reload within 30 minutes is the same visit). When that is unknown, or newer than the lead's last report to you (meta/lead_checkin), the window starts at that report instead. Each card is listed once under the last move it made. Released is its own group, since those cards were listed as built when they merged. For the apps still in the vault, only cards built in the window.
                • In flight: cards In progress or In review, most recently changed first. "Quiet" marks a card with no change for a day while In review, or three days while In progress. Cards that need you, cards in the license stages and vault cards are never marked quiet; a vault card's state is as of the last sync.
                • Left before the license: stage 0 and 1 cards that aren't built, per app, the ones being worked on first, then by priority. It counts stages 0 and 1 together, while a tile in a stage 0 or 1 phase counts that phase only.
                • Built: status Done (merged into dev) or Released (in main through a dev to main release). The percent tracks the build, not the shipping.
                • Phase: the lowest stage that still has unfinished cards (After 1.0 last).
                • The tile's big number is the one in each app's block of the vault's Dashboard (_scripts/app-progress): while the phase is stage 0 or 1, built cards over all cards in that phase; once only license stages are left, "Before the license": built cards over every stage 0 and 1 card. The tile then also lists the licensed phase's own x/y (the number the Dashboard's top summary tiles show).
                • Wait on the license: unfinished cards in stages 2 and 3 (they need the paid developer program), every phase.
                • In review, in progress: cards with that status, every stage.
                • Need release: Done cards that have a PR, waiting on the next dev to main release.
                • Needs you this phase: needs_j true, not built, in the current phase. Only a YAML true counts, as in Obsidian.
                • Cards built per day: the day a card first moved to Done or Released, from the card's history in the database or the vault's git history. Cards migrated from Notion already built are left out.
                • Where each app's 1.0 cards stand: every card in stages 0 to 3 (After 1.0 left out), by status.
                • These are the rules of the vault's Dashboard note (_scripts/summary and _scripts/app-progress), so the mirrored apps match Obsidian at the commit named on their tile. The mirror is only as fresh as its last sync.
